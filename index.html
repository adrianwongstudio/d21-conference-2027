Welcome

Three days of contests, keynotes and shared meals

Every spring, Toastmasters District 21 hosts a three-day conference for members from across British Columbia. The program includes the District-level speech contests, keynote speakers, education sessions and five shared meals.

Over one weekend, we’ll sharpen the way we communicate, cultivate the leaders and clubs who make District 21 strong, and celebrate everything our members have achieved this year. And because Distinguished is only a milestone, we’ll also look ahead to what lies beyond.

Sponsorship

Put your brand in front of 300+ professionals

Sponsors reach professionals, managers and business owners from across British Columbia. Your money also funds a non-profit that trains people to speak and lead with confidence. Toastmasters values integrity, respect, service and excellence, and we look for sponsors who share them.

  • Reach an engaged audience: professionals, entrepreneurs, managers and community leaders who pay to improve themselves.
  • Three days of visibility: your brand appears at meals, contests, keynotes and in the printed program from Friday to Sunday.
  • Community goodwill: we thank sponsors from the stage and in print.
  • Lasting exposure: attendees keep the program book, and the conference website stays online after the event.
  • Built-in value: Silver and above include full-conference tickets worth $349 each for your team or clients.
